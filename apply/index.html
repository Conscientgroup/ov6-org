Veteran intake

You earned this.

Join the interest list for ownership pathways in Sussex County. This is not a lottery and not a rental waitlist — it is the first step in a development pipeline.

What we screen

  • Honorable or general discharge with a DD-214 (or equivalent)
  • Income eligibility is reviewed with you — not one cutoff for the whole county
  • Homeownership-readiness assessment with OV6
  • Willingness to live in the inaugural Sussex County theater
  • We help however we can — no "Sorry you don't qualify."

  1. Step 01

    Qualify

    Verified veterans complete OV6's intake — DD-214 review and a homeownership readiness assessment. Sussex County, Delaware is the first theater.

  2. Step 02

    Move In

    Qualified veterans move into homes built using the right delivery method for the family — on OV6-developed lots.

  3. Step 03

    Build Equity

    Veterans build equity in their homes and may qualify for other opportunities.
