Mission decode

What OV6 actually is

Overwatch Veterans 6 Inc. is not a rental-voucher charity and not a tech demo. It is a Delaware 501(c)(3) public charity (Public Charity 509(a)(2), EIN 33-4737345) that develops real estate veterans can own. The inaugural theater is Sussex County. The unit of impact is a family with equity — protected by a permanent affordability mechanism so the next veteran can enter too.

Multiple delivery methods (custom, modular, 3D-printed concrete, and more) are not a gimmick. They are a matching engine: permanence vs. speed vs. next-gen cost. Wraparound services (VA navigation, literacy, employment) keep ownership from becoming a foreclosure statistic. Free AI websites, CRM, and a business suite treat the veteran as an operator, not a beneficiary.

SussexCounty.ai is the quiet strategic asset: parcel, permit, deed, zoning, NOAA. Site selection is usually the place housing nonprofits bleed years. OV6 is positioning to pick lots like a developer with a satellite.

The one-line that should own every surface: We don't house veterans. We make them owners and investors.

Live-site gaps

Audit of ov6.org as of 21 Aug 2026. These are product defects, not taste notes.

Critical

The live site is a single Babel-in-browser SPA

React, ReactDOM, and Babel Standalone load from CDN. First paint waits on three third-party scripts plus a 69KB inline app. Crawlers, grant reviewers, and veterans on weak LTE see a noscript card — not the mission. Google's snippet currently indexes only the fallback, which is why ov6.org looks empty in search.

Critical

Zero photographic proof of the work

A real-estate + construction nonprofit with 80+ lots, multiple delivery methods, and a 3DCP thesis currently ships no drone, no site, no unit, no family. Donors fund what they can see. Grant officers fund what they can verify. The current page is type on linen.

High

Donation amount is theater

The $6 / $25 / $60 / $150 chooser and monthly toggle never reach Stripe. The hosted Payment Link is a single SKU. That is a conversion leak and a trust leak — the veteran who selects $150 still lands on a generic checkout.

High

Dead navigation and social chrome

Footer programs, volunteer, partner, press, careers, and six social tiles are non-links. Interest-list POST hits /api/waitlist that is not a durable product surface. Placeholders read as unfinished to a DD-214 holder and to a program officer.

High

Mission is compressed into one sentence

The real thesis — owners not tenants, multiple delivery methods, free AI for vet businesses, Sussex County program points, permanent affordability — is present but not sequenced. Visitors cannot tell if OV6 is a housing charity, a developer, a tech nonprofit, or a fund.

Med

No proof stack for institutional capital

EIN, 501(c)(3), and 509(a)(2) are correctly stated. Missing: Form 990 link, board, conflict policy, impact metrics with sources, lot map, construction timeline, VA loan / builder ID status, and a one-page capability statement.

Ranked Delta lifts

Sequence, don't boil the ocean. D1–D4 are this prototype. D5–D8 are the 90-day operating plan.

D1

Cinematic proof layer

Hero drone, method boards, 3DCP orbit, blue-hour village, family stills. Photography is the product for a developer. This prototype ships that layer.

Donor conversion + grant credibility

D2

Two-path information architecture

Keep the dual CTA (I served / I support) above the fold AND as a dedicated board. Never mix eligibility copy with donate copy in the same paragraph.

Veteran intake vs. donor yield

D3

Stripe Checkout with price_data

Replace the Payment Link with Checkout Sessions that honor amount + interval. Pass $amt and monthly as line items. Add Apple Pay / Google Pay. Recurring is the endowment.

Gift size + monthly retention

D4

Server-rendered pages, not Babel

This rebuild is the model: static/SSR, real <title> and schema, noscript that matches the brand, no 400kb of runtime compile. Target LCP < 2.5s on 4G.

SEO, LCP, accessibility

D5

Proof for capital

Publish: board roster, 990, lot map of the 40 inaugural, program-point methodology, affordability covenant summary, construction method matrix with cost/sqft ranges, and a one-pager for county council.

Foundation and CDFI underwriting

D6

Veterans as operators, not beneficiaries

Lead with ownership, investment, and free AI. Housing is the chassis. Ensuring real change is the mission. Repeat that sentence until it is the search snippet.

Brand distinction vs. HUD / HFOT / SSVF

D7

SussexCounty.ai as a product, not a footnote

Give the intelligence layer its own board: live parcel, permit, zoning. That's the NVIDIA Inception story made visible — and the reason OV6 is not a generic housing nonprofit.

Site-selection moat

D8

Interest list as a CRM, not a form

Segment on submit: veteran-home / veteran-business / donor / partner. Route to different drip sequences. Currently one blob. That's a 4x yield change with no new traffic.

Pipeline

90-day operating plan

  1. 01

    Days 1–14 · Ship this surface

    Replace Babel SPA with this next-gen property. Keep EIN, Stripe, GA4 consent, privacy, terms. Add real drone of the Sussex lots as soon as a camera is on site — generated UHD is a placeholder, not a substitute for the actual dirt.

  2. 02

    Days 15–30 · Make money move

    Checkout Sessions with amount + interval. Recurring default. $6 is a literacy session; keep it, but add $500 / $1,000 lot-sponsor tiers for family offices. Wire Apple Pay.

  3. 03

    Days 31–60 · Proof pack

    Board page, 990, lot map, method cost matrix, program-point worksheet, affordability covenant one-pager, partner logos (with written permission), and a two-minute founder film shot on the first lot.

  4. 04

    Days 61–90 · Pipeline

    Segmented CRM from the interest list. Veteran-business AI waitlist as a separate product page. County-council brief. NVIDIA Inception story told as infrastructure, not a footer badge.
